The new imageleft.com is live. This post explains the why and the how.

## Why now

The old site was a Next.js project mixing marketing and back-office concerns. The new
imageleft.com is a static Astro site with content driven by an adapter. That means
faster deploys, sharper pages, and a clean separation between what users see and what
the team manages.

## What changed

- Static-first. No SSR. Pages render at build, served as plain HTML.
- Content adapter. Posts, jobs, and customer stories share a typed interface so the
  next iteration can swap to an HTTP API without touching call sites.
- A real design system. Tokens, primitives, and sections all live in one place.
