Most early teams don't fail from lack of vision. They fail from the gaps between what they
imagined and what they actually built.

## The three gaps

There's the **product gap** between idea and prototype. The **engineering gap** between
prototype and production. And the **operational gap** between launch and scale.

The teams that ship treat each one as a system to close, not a phase to wait out.
